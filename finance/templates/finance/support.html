How does the month rollover work?▼
When you view a new month for the first time, MoneyMate automatically copies your recurring income sources and fixed bills from the previous month. One-off and variable bills stay behind.
How do I track a one-off expense?▼
Go to Bills, add a new bill and set the Frequency to "One-off". It'll count for that month only and won't roll over.
What's the difference between Fixed and Variable bills?▼
Fixed bills have the same amount every month (like rent or a subscription). Variable bills change month to month (like a utility bill based on usage). Only fixed bills roll over automatically.
How are savings goal timelines calculated?▼
MoneyMate divides your remaining amount (target minus current) by your monthly contribution to estimate how many months until you hit your goal. Update your contribution any time to recalculate.
How do I change my password or email?▼
Click your username in the top-right corner and go to Settings. You'll find tabs for updating your password and email address.
Can I delete my account?▼
Yes — go to Settings → Danger Zone. You'll be asked to type your username to confirm. This permanently deletes all your data and cannot be undone.
